# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 September 2026_

**PCBPad does not collect any personal data.**

## What stays on your device

Everything. Your boards, schematics, libraries, settings and recovery snapshots are
stored on your device (and in iCloud only if you yourself keep your files there).
PCBPad has no server, no account, and no login.

## What is never sent anywhere

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
- No usage statistics.
- No board or schematic files.

PCBPad uses Apple's MetricKit to record crash and performance reports. **These reports
are written to a local file on your device and are never transmitted.** You can export
them yourself from the app if you want to send one to us.

## When something does leave your device

Only when you choose it, and only by your own action:

- **Feedback.** If you tap "Send feedback", PCBPad opens your mail app with a message
  already written, addressed to lolojdard@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you send it.
- **External links.** Links such as the Ko-fi page open in your browser. Those sites
  have their own privacy policies.

## Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are handled entirely by Apple. PCBPad never sees
or stores your payment details.

## Children

PCBPad is a professional design tool and is not directed at children.
